# Velocity Rush: Harbor City — Story Walkthrough

All characters, crews and places are original and fictional.

## Cast

| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| **Ghostline** (you) | A newcomer who outran the Tidewater Saints on the ring road. |
| **Mara Quill** | Mechanic, runs the Lantern Garage. Your mentor and sponsor. |
| **Dex Okafor** | Late-night radio voice and info broker of the night scene. |
| **Juno Vale** | Boss of the **Tidewater Saints** (docks). Act I boss. |
| **Kade "Gridlock" Moreno** | Boss of the **Circuit Choir** (downtown). Act II boss. |
| **Sable Ishikawa** | Boss of the **Dust Ravens** (canyon & foundry). Act III boss. |
| **Commissioner Elias Harrow** | Head of the Harbor Interceptor Unit. Final boss. |
| **Lio Quill** | Mara's brother, a racing legend who vanished three years ago. |

## Prologue

At 2:14 a.m. Dex sets the scene: four crews run Harbor City, and every one of them owes its crown to Lio Quill, who has been missing for three years. Mara takes you into the Lantern Garage. You race for her, and she hopes that winning will make the crews tell her what happened to her brother.

You start with the cheapest car in the catalog (Class D) and $12,000.

## Act I — Low Tide (Harbor Loop)

**Story:** Juno Vale's Tidewater Saints run the docks like a church. Vale lets you onto her loop to see whether you are worth a seat at her table.

| # | Event | Type | Track | Objective | Reward | Unlocks after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Harbor Welcome | Circuit, 3 laps, 5 rivals | harbor-loop | Finish top 3 | $4,000 / 100 REP | — |
| 2 | Dockside Dash | Sprint, 5 rivals | harbor-loop | Finish 1st | $5,000 / 120 | 1 |
| 3 | Crane Yard Drift | Drift, 2 laps | industrial-zone | 8,000 pts | $4,500 / 110 | 1 |
| 4 | Tide Clock | Time Trial, 2 laps | harbor-loop | Under 3:10 | $5,000 / 120 | 2 |
| 5 | **BOSS: Sermon on the Loop** | Circuit, 5 laps vs 3 | harbor-loop | Beat Juno Vale (1st) | $15,000 / 400 | 2, 3, 4 |

**Recommended:** your starter car, then Tires L1 ($2,500) and Engine L1 ($4,000). After Dockside Dash, think about a Class C car (Comet Sprint for grip, or Vandal Strike for power).

**Tips:**
- Harbor Loop has long straights between the docks. Brake early for the slow container-yard hairpin, then use nitro on the exit.
- For Crane Yard Drift, link the slides together. The combo multiplier rises while you stay sideways and falls fast once you grip up. Wall hits end a chain.
- For Tide Clock, aim for clean laps. The target allows about 95 s per lap.
- Vale defends the inside line. Pass her on the exit of the final corner by going in late and coming out fast.

**Outro beat:** Vale admits that Lio raced her here once, and that before he left he gave "something" to Kade downtown. **Act II unlocks**, along with Class B cars and Level 2 upgrades.

## Act II — Signal Noise (Downtown Grid)

**Story:** Kade Moreno's Circuit Choir treat downtown as a stage, and Kade times every light cycle to the second. Harrow's Interceptors also show up for the first time.

| # | Event | Type | Track | Objective | Reward | Unlocks after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Grid Tempo | Circuit, 3 laps, 7 rivals | downtown-grid | Top 3 | $8,000 / 160 | — |
| 7 | Signal Knockout | Knockout, 4 laps, 4 rivals | downtown-grid | Last car standing | $10,000 / 200 | 6 |
| 8 | First Heat | Pursuit | downtown-grid | Survive 90 s or escape | $9,000 / 220 | 6 |
| 9 | Overpass Serenade | Drift, 2 laps | downtown-grid | 14,000 pts | $8,500 / 180 | 6 |
| 10 | **BOSS: Final Movement** | Circuit, 4 laps vs 3 | downtown-grid | Beat Kade (1st) | $25,000 / 600 | 7, 8, 9 |
| S1 | Midnight Knockout (side) | Knockout, 4 laps | harbor-loop | Last car standing | $7,000 / 120 | Act II |

**Recommended:** a Class B car. Kestrel RS handles best on the 90° grid corners, and Thunderclap 77 is fastest on the avenues. Get Engine L2 and Tires L2.

**Tips:**
- On the grid, short-shift out of the right-angle corners. Too much throttle in a low gear just spins the wheels.
- In Knockout, you only need to avoid last place each lap. Don't take risks while you are safely mid-pack.
- In First Heat, the heat meter rises while police can see you. Break line of sight around downtown blocks, or just stay ahead until the timer runs out.

**Outro beat:** Kade gives you Lio's transmitter, which pings the canyon. He warns that Harrow's units have been circling ever since Lio vanished. Dex realises the police are running a stakeout. **Act III unlocks**, along with Class A cars and Level 3 upgrades.

## Act III — Dust & Static (Canyon Run & Industrial Zone)

**Story:** Outside the city, Sable Ishikawa's Dust Ravens drift the canyon switchbacks and move contraband through the old foundry. Sable knows about "Lio's deal".

| # | Event | Type | Track | Objective | Reward | Unlocks after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Canyon Descent | Sprint, 5 rivals | canyon-run | 1st | $14,000 / 250 | — |
| 12 | Switchback Smoke | Drift, 2 laps | canyon-run | 20,000 pts | $13,000 / 240 | — |
| 13 | Foundry Knockout | Knockout, 5 laps, 5 rivals | industrial-zone | Last car standing | $16,000 / 280 | 11 |
| 14 | Ledger Run | Pursuit | industrial-zone | Survive 120 s or escape | $15,000 / 300 | 12 |
| 15 | **BOSS: Raven's Bargain** | Circuit, 3 laps vs 3 | canyon-run | Beat Sable (1st) | $40,000 / 900 | 13, 14 |
| S2 | Foundry Clock (side) | Time Trial, 2 laps | industrial-zone | Under 2:40 | $9,000 / 150 | Act III |

**Recommended:** a Class A car. Nightjar Vector suits the canyon. Halcyon Apex is better if you mostly want top speed. Fit Weight Reduction L2+, because the canyon rewards a light, agile car.

**Tips:**
- The canyon has elevation changes. Brake in a straight line before each crest.
- For Switchback Smoke, use a short handbrake tap to start each slide, then balance it on the throttle.
- The foundry lanes are narrow, so wall contact costs a lot. In Ledger Run, use the long loading-bay straights to build a gap.

**Outro beat / the first twist:** Sable reveals that Lio made a deal with Commissioner Harrow, trading the crews' routes for a clean record. Then she reveals that he fed Harrow **fake** routes for a year and ran when Harrow found out. Dex asks the key question: *"Ghostline... Mara, who gave you that callsign?"* **Act IV unlocks**, along with Class S cars.

## Act IV — Skyline (Skyline Sprint)

**The twist:** Mara admits that she gave you the callsign. On your first night she found Lio's old radio tag under your dash, because **the car you bought used to be Lio's**. A message Lio recorded earlier plays over the radio and names **Harrow as the real power behind every crew in Harbor City**. Harrow sets each crew's schedule and protects them in exchange for control. The Saints, the Choir and the Ravens all join you.

| # | Event | Type | Track | Objective | Reward | Unlocks after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | Lio's Line | Time Trial, 1 lap | skyline-sprint | Under 1:35 | $20,000 / 300 | — |
| 17 | Crew Summit | Circuit, 3 laps, 7 rivals | skyline-sprint | 1st | $30,000 / 500 | 16 |
| 18 | Interceptor Gauntlet | Pursuit | skyline-sprint | Survive 150 s or escape | $30,000 / 600 | 16 |
| 19 | **FINAL BOSS: The Schedule Breaks** | Sprint vs Harrow | skyline-sprint | Beat Harrow | $100,000 / 2,000 | 17, 18 |

**Recommended:** a Class S car (Zenith Phantom X or Aurora Eclipse) with every upgrade at Level 3. Nitro L3 is the most important one for the final sprint.

**Tips:**
- The Skyline is the fastest track in the game. Use nitro on the bridge straights, not on corner exits where grip is limited.
- During the final race, Lio radios a tip: *stay on the inside at the tower hairpin*. Harrow always leaves it open.
- In Interceptor Gauntlet, heat starts high. Keep your speed up and use the elevated sections to break line of sight.

**Ending:** The Interceptor unit is grounded and Harrow's files are broadcast across the harbor. Lio comes out of hiding at the north pier, Mara gets her brother back, and the streets belong to the racers again. Vale asks: *"Same time tomorrow night?"* Events stay replayable at 50% payout, and challenges stay open.

## Challenges (any time)

Lifetime challenges are Redline Rookie (200 km/h), Harbor Missile (280 km/h), Skyline Bullet (330 km/h), Smoke Signal (20k drift), Raven Feather (50k drift), Ghost Protocol (3 escapes), Crew Favourite (10 wins), Long Night (100 km), Bottled Lightning (120 s of nitro) and Clean Lines (10 clean laps). Two daily challenges are also picked from a rotating pool each day. Claim the rewards on the Challenges screen.
